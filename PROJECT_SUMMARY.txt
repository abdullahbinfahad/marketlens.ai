================================================================================
SCORING ALGORITHMS:
================================================================================

DEMAND SCORE (0-100):
  Formula: (Search_Volume × 0.35) + (Sales_Velocity × 0.40) + 
           (Reviews × 0.15) + (Trend × 0.10)
  Measures: Market appetite and growth potential

PROFIT SCORE (0-100):
  Formula: (Price_Competitiveness × 0.40) + (Rating × 0.35) + 
           (Competition_Efficiency × 0.25)
  Measures: Financial viability and margin potential

RISK SCORE (0-100):
  Formula: (Market_Saturation × 0.50) + (Competitor_Penalty × 0.35) + 
           (Rating_Risk × 0.15)
  Measures: Market barriers and saturation

OVERALL SCORE (0-100):
  Formula: (Demand × 0.40) + (Profit × 0.30) - (Risk × 0.20) + 
           (Competition × 0.10)
  Verdicts:
    • GO: ≥75 (Strong opportunity)
    • CAUTION: 50-74 (Moderate opportunity)
    • PASS: <50 (High risk)
